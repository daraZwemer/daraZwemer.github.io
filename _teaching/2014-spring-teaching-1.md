I was the graduate teaching assistant for Dr. Evan Jordan. I helped mark drafts of each part of a research paper and then the entire research manuscript at the end of the year. We also helped students develop skills searching for papers and understanding research manuscripts through introductory assignments. I provided rich feedback to ensure students would be able to approach research writing confidently in their future careers.
